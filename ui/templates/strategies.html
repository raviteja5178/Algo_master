📊 Standard EMA Crossover Active All Day
Evaluates 5-minute EMA9 / EMA21 alignment, close breakout, and 5-minute trend confirmation. Active from 09:15 to 15:00 IST.
  • 5m Close > EMA21 (or < for Put)
  • 5m EMA9 > EMA21 (or < for Put)
  • 5m Close > Previous Candle High (or < Low for Put)
  • 5m Trend Confirmation check
⚡ Opening Range Breakout (ORB) 5M High/Low
Fires immediately when the 5-minute candle close crosses the opening 5-minute range (09:15–09:20). Highly effective for morning momentum.
  • CE: Close(0) crosses ABOVE Opening Range High
  • PE: Close(0) crosses BELOW Opening Range Low
  • Ensures instant breakouts right after the opening candle closes
⏰ Time Range Breakout (TRB) 15M High/Low Breakout
Fires when the completed 5-minute candle close crosses the High or Low of the 15-minute anchor candle formed between 09:45 AM and 10:00 AM.
  • Anchor Candle: 09:45 (15-minute interval)
  • CE_TRB: 5m Close crosses ABOVE 15m high boundary
  • PE_TRB: 5m Close crosses BELOW 15m low boundary
  • Time Guard: Evaluation is strictly blocked before 10:00 AM IST
🧱 Order Block (OB) Price Zone Re-entry
Fires CE_OB / PE_OB when the 5m candle close enters a valid (non-breaker) bullish or bearish order block zone detected on the 5m chart. Logic mirrors the Flux Charts "Volumized Order Blocks" Pine Script indicator.
  • Swing detection: ATR-capped pivot highs/lows (swing length: 10)
  • CE_OB: close inside a valid bullish OB (formed when price crossed above a swing high)
  • PE_OB: close inside a valid bearish OB (formed when price crossed below a swing low)
  • Invalidation: Wick method — OB becomes a breaker when pierced, then deleted
  • Max OB zones tracked: 3 per side — OBs larger than ATR × 3.5 discarded
🚀 Momentum Phase 2 Afternoon Boost
Fires during the afternoon breakout window (13:30–14:30) on large-body breakout candles, even if the price does not break the previous high.
  • Candle body size ≥ minimum required points (default 40.0)
  • 5m Close > EMA9, and EMA9 > EMA21
  • 5m Trend Confirmation
🌊 VWAP Retest Intraday Mean Reversion
Fires CE_VWAP / PE_VWAP when price pulls back to VWAP and a 5-minute candle closes back on the right side with momentum. Institutions use VWAP as a benchmark — large orders cluster around it.
  • CE_VWAP: At least one of the last 3 candles closed BELOW VWAP (retest dip)
  • CE_VWAP: Current 5m close > VWAP
  • CE_VWAP: Current 5m close > previous candle high (momentum confirmation)
  • PE_VWAP: Mirror — retest pop above VWAP then close back below with momentum
  • Min bounce distance above/below VWAP: 0 pts
📅 Previous Day High / Low (PDHL) Breakout Key Level Breakout
Fires CE_PDHL / PE_PDHL when the 5-minute candle closes beyond the previous day's high or low. These are widely watched institutional levels with strong breakout follow-through.
  • CE_PDHL: Previous candle closed at/below PDH — fresh crossover (not continuation)
  • CE_PDHL: Current 5m close > PDH + 0 pts buffer
  • CE_PDHL: Current 5m close > previous candle high (momentum confirmation)
  • PE_PDHL: Mirror — fresh close below PDL with momentum confirmation
📉 NATR Trailing Stop ATR Trend Flip
Fires CE_NATR / PE_NATR when the 5-minute close crosses the dynamic ATR trailing stop line. Mirrors the "ATR 5 Min Index Study" (NATRPeriod=21, NATRMultip=3.0). The stop ratchets up in uptrends and down in downtrends — a crossover signals a trend flip.
  • ATR Trailing Stop = ATR(21) x 3.0 — ratchets with price
  • CE_NATR: previous close was at/below stop AND current close crosses above stop
  • PE_NATR: previous close was at/above stop AND current close crosses below stop
  • Minimum 21 + 3 candles required to seed the ATR
💡 ATR Copilot Band Breakout
Fires CE_ATR / PE_ATR when the 5-minute candle closes outside the ATR noise band for the first time. Bands = EMA(21) ± ATR(5) × 3.0. Price must have been inside the band on the previous candle (fresh breakout only — no continuation entries).
  • CE_ATR: previous close ≤ upper band AND current close > upper band
  • PE_ATR: previous close ≥ lower band AND current close < lower band
  • No-trade zone: price between lower and upper band (noise)
  • R:R gate — CE requires ≥ 2.0, PE requires ≥ 1.2
  • Minimum 21 + 5 + 2 candles required to seed indicators
