🎯 Recommendation

Theme 1 (Terminal Pro) is the right choice. It's a surgical refinement of what you already have — same dark palette, same layout DNA, zero migration risk.

What to upgrade from your current UI: Add the sl_mode badge to the header, add the Swing Levels block to the sidebar, surface R:R in the KPI bar, add the Risk Track Bar to the right column, and add the swing high/low dashed lines to the chart. That's ~50 lines of HTML — no backend changes needed since the data already exists in the RISK_PARAMS_APPLIED log and /api/status.
